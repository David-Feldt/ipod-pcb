Verdict: retain the existing boot/reset circuit

With stable power and default recovery-related eFuses, the design provides normal flash boot and manual ROM recovery independently of application firmware.

Recovery: hold BOOT, press and release RESET, keep BOOT held briefly after releasing RESET, then release BOOT and flash. UART fallback: use a 3.3 V logic adapter, adapter RX → TP1/TX, adapter TX → TP2/RX, GND → TP3. Leave adapter power disconnected when the board is already powered.

Not a guarantee against damaged hardware or irreversible eFuse configuration. Pending bring-up: repeated cold boots and rapid power cycling, EN/3.3 V waveforms, USB ROM recovery and manual UART recovery. No extra DTR/RTS auto-reset circuitry is fitted.
